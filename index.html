Health Sunday

On Sunday, 10th August 2025, Health Sunday was observed across all Presbyterian Churches. At Love Congregation, New Legon Oko, the celebration featured an insightful health talk on “Understanding and Supporting Autism Awareness in Our Communities.”

The guest speaker, Mr. Benjamin Asiamah Kuffour, father to Renate Nana Yaa Kuffour, who is on the autism spectrum, and co-founder of the autism support centre Klicks Africa, shared a powerful combination of personal experiences and expert knowledge on supporting children and families living with autism.

Mr. Kuffour explained that Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) is a neurodevelopmental condition that affects communication, social interaction, and behaviour. He underscored the need to cultivate inclusive attitudes, implement effective interventions, and build strong community support systems to improve the lives of individuals and families affected by autism.

Grounding his message in Christian teaching, Mr. Kuffour reminded the congregation of the Church’s divine mandate. Referring to Jesus’ command to “go and make disciples of all nations,” he emphasized that this mission includes children with autism. He encouraged the Church to create awareness, foster a sense of belonging, and include them fully in all activities. As he put it, “Children with autism are part of the Great Commission and must experience the love and care that Christ demonstrated.”

He concluded with a call to action: to increase education and awareness about autism, ensuring that persons on the spectrum are treated with dignity, equality, and the same rights and privileges as everyone else.

Written by Mrs. Benedicta Adubea Quartey

Momentum Summit & Cookout 2025

The Momentum Summit Committee and Cookout Cpmmittee was made up of 18 persons and 17 sub committee members which was chaired by Mrs. Benedicta Adubea Quartey and Mrs. Emelia Barkey Djanie. The committees managed to secure 8 sponsorships for cookout. These sponsors were Stanjulingra Bakery, BC Creation,RAM Multimedia ,Blue Skies Company, Peeva Water, Peeva Drinks, Tasty Tom Tomatoe Paste and Spices and Onua TV.

Theme for Momentum Summit 2025 was OUR FATHER (Matthew 6:9) we were blessed with five great men and woman of God thus: Rev. Semaha Kodjo, Rev. Mrs. Patricia Addy, Rev. Clifford Robert Amane- Sarpong, Rev. Emmanuel Afriyie Amoako and Rev.Dr. Joseph Kofi Aidoo.

The program was scheduled from Monday 30th June, 2025 to 4th July 2025 for 5 day revival dubbed Momentum Summit and Saturday 5th July, 2025 with Inter Regional Cookout. The programs were climaxed with a thanksgiving service which a customized cake with momentum Summit logo beautifully attached to thank all Church members for their enormous support for momentum Summit and Cookout over the past five years.

Excerpts from Day 1 to Day 5 of the revival are as follows;

 Day 1- Monday 30th June, 2025- Rev Semaha Kodjo commenced as speaker for day 1. We had total attendance of 177 which entailed 81 males and 96 females. Key notes from his sermon were : Delays can’t stop God’s plan for you. Do not let people who don’t understand or know your destiny limit you.

 Day 2 – Tuesday 1st July,2025 – Rev. Mrs. Patricia Addy. Attendance was 233 thus 110 males and 123 females. Keynotes from her sermon were: What we call delay is God’s preparatory stage to get us to a glorious end. The Lord’s prayer is amodel prayer that shows us the ingredients that should go into prayer. Our Father is a call for unity, oneness and not leaving anyone behind.

 Day 3- Wednesday 2nd July, 2025 - Rev. Clifford Robert Amane Sarpong. Attendance was 248 thus 121 males and 127 females. Keynotes from his sermon were: He taught them to acknowledge firstly that God is father. A father is a source of life.. God sustains us through his power and presence. Our father is calling us to come home and have an intimate relationship with him.

 Day 4- Thursday 3rd July,2025 – Rev. Emmanuel Afriyie Amoako. Attendance was 3 15 thus 150 males and 165 females. Keynotes from his sermon were: The model prayer is actually about God, he is the prayer. Our father is the only person who gives us daily bread from cradle to grave. What you pray for is more important than who you pray to. Hallowing the name of the Lord must reflect the pre- prayer, preparation, worship and thanksgiving.

 Day 5 – Friday 4th July, 2025- Rev. Dr. Joseph Kofi Aidoo. Attendance was 451 thus 201 males and 250 females. Keynotes from his sermon were: Our father does not have an off day, you can call on him at all times. Sometimes to help you focus in prayer you need to look up to Heaven to the one who hears you while you pray.

 Day 6 – Saturday 5th July, 2025- Inter Regional Cookout. Attendance was five hundred (500). All ethnic groups came out massively to showcase their rich Ghanaian delicacies which were made right from scratch into the bellies of the audiences in attendance. We had 7 judges in all thus Rev. Dr. Joseph Kofi Aidoo, Rev. Clifford Robert Amane Sarpong (Salem Congregation, Taifa Mr. Adjei), Cat. Mrs. Bernice Djan, Patricia Siawbea Addy esq. (Redemption Congregation. Pokuase), Mr. Felix Nii-Boi Quartey, Mrs. Emelia Djanie and Mr. Emmanuel Nortey.

Indeed through the help of Elohim both programs were well organized and well attended.

The Church Choir

Indeed, determination is the fuel to success.

The Church Choir of the Presbyterian Church of Ghana, New Legon-Oko Congregation, successfully held its maiden edition of Hymns on the Street on Friday, 27th June 2025. Although initially scheduled for Thursday, 26th June,2025 the event was rescheduled due to heavy rainfall. By the grace of God, it was held the following day at the forecourt of the Superior Medical Centre, located in New Legon Oko.

The evening was filled with spirit-lifting Presbyterian hymns, each rendered with deep reverence and accompanied by the rich histories behind their composition. Hymns performed included:

These hymns were ministered in both Twi and Ga, allowing many in the community to connect deeply through familiar languages.
Mr. Daniel Narh Ogum shared a powerful exhortation from Ephesians 5:15–20 and 2 Corinthians 5:10, reminding all present to live wisely and prepare their hearts for the Lord. The altar call was made with a stirring performance by the Presby Singing Band, singing "Wɛ siesie wo ho anaa?" ; a song that calls us to reflect on our readiness for Christ's return.

In addition to the hymns, uplifting Highlife gospel songs were also shared, including:

The event was a joyful experience for both those present and those who joined us online via our Facebook Live stream. Viewers expressed their joy through comments, with many even requesting their favorite hymns.

We express our heartfelt gratitude to Dr. Nasigri Mahama, Head of the Superior Medical Centre, and his dedicated team for granting us the space and support to minister through music and the Word.

May all who participated continue to be filled with joy, reflection, and renewed commitment to the Lord.

Men's Week Celebration

The Men’s Week Celebration began on Wednesday, 11th June 2025, with an engaging Exposition on the theme: “Celebrating Our Heritage, Persisting in Missions: Men, Go Labour On.” 1 Corinthians 15: 8, 2 Timothy 4: 1-5, Matthew 9: 35.

This opening event laid a strong foundation for a meaningful week of spiritual growth, leadership, and service.

Day 2 featured an Entrepreneurship Talk focused on how to start and manage a business. Attendees also gained valuable insights into Customer Service and Digital Marketing, equipping them with essential tools for personal and professional development.

Day 3 was devoted to a Prayer Session, where heartfelt prayers were offered for families, the Presbyterian Church across the nation, the leadership of the Church and the country, and for men everywhere. It was a solemn and spiritually uplifting experience.

On Day 4, the Men’s Fellowship led a Clean-Up Exercise, demonstrating their commitment to service and environmental stewardship. The initiative fostered teamwork and reinforced the value of caring for God’s creation.

The week culminated on Day 5 with a joyful Thanksgiving Service, which also marked the celebration of Father’s Day. The Men’s Fellowship blessed the congregation with their signature hymn, PH 42 – “Anounyam ne Aseda Yɛ Osoro Nyankopɔn de daa Mo mo... Sɛ wo Yɛ saa wura,” offering praise and gratitude to God.

The 2025 Men’s Week Celebration was a memorable and successful event, marked by spiritual renewal, empowerment, and fellowship.

Written by Mrs. Benedicta Adubea Quartey

Lilian Yeboah Wadie Block

On Sunday 9th March, 2025 the Lilian Yeboah Wadie Block was dedicated by Rev. Col. David Addoteye Asare (GA Presbytery Chairperson), assisted by Rev. Isaac Quist ( GA Presbytery Clerk) and Rev. Dr. Joseph Kofi Aidoo (Minister- In- Charge) in the name of the Truine God.

Other ministers and District reps present were Rev. Patrick Nii Owusu Armah (PCG Property Manager), Rev. Clifford Amane Sarpong (PCG, Salem Congregation), Mr. Meshach Asah-Kisseidu (Taifa District Lay Rep), Mr. Bernard Buertey Okutu (Taifa District Clerk).

This beautiful edifice was erected by Mrs. Lilian E. Yeboah Wadie & family to commiserate Mrs. Wadie's 70th Birthday and also to thank God for his marvelous blessings upon her life. The building entails Children's place of service, Brigade Office with instruments and washroom.

To God be the Glory!

Written by Mrs. Benedicta Adubea Quartey
